A recommendation is a category-A-recommendation and is an evidence-type-1-recommendation.
If a patient has an opioid-use-disorder then every clinician should offer an evidence-based-medication-treatment for the opioid-use-disorder to the patient.
If a patient has an opioid-use-disorder and there is a clinician then it is recommended that the clinician does not use a medication-free-detoxification for the patient.
Every medication-free-detoxification increases a risk for a drug-use-resumption and increases a risk for an overdose and increases a risk for an overdose-death.
