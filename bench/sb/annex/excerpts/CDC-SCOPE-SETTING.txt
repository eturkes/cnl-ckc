If a patient receives a hospital-care and the patient does not provably receive a pain-management-prescription at a facility-discharge then it is false that a recommendation applies to the patient.
If a patient receives an emergency-department-care and the patient does not provably receive a pain-management-prescription at a facility-discharge then it is false that a recommendation applies to the patient.
If a patient receives an observational-setting-care with a possible-inpatient-admission and the patient does not provably receive a pain-management-prescription at a facility-discharge then it is false that a recommendation applies to the patient.
