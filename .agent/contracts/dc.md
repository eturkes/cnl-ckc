## Design

D1 Admission. The scope pass's reasons `deontic_condition`, `negated_deontic`, `root_deontic` retire (cs1 D4 keeps `root`, `subject`, `consequent`, in that order, on the shapes they name today); the supersession pass's `root_deontic` reject retires, and with it the class `supersession_shape` (no reason remains). Their kernel `Why` + `Sym` variants, spec literals + byte proofs retire together (`m6_symbols` regenerated by `.agent/archive/generators/m6-symbols`). Preserved: every other reject + its order, including the translation boundary `deferred_operator(antecedent, Op, S)` for any operator inside a `does not provably` condition, in every document (`v1_flatten_cond`, `ace_to_pl.pl:3715–3722`; spec `flatten_cond`, `emit.rs:379–382`; E2). A retired probe compiles or reports its next surviving reason (e.g. `order-condition-subject` → `subject`, `order-root-deontic` → `root`).

D2 Statement guard (replaces cs1 D5). Deontic group = an emitted variant group (one DRS variant: each arm of a Horn split = its own group; a root sentence = its root group) with a clause whose head is a should|must|may edge (`guideline_operator(_, _, Op)`, Op ∈ {should, must, may}, any depth: under `can`, `-` or another box) or whose body holds a positive `guideline_operator(_, _, Op)` goal (a condition's box, nested under `can` or `-` included; a box inside NAF never reaches emission: D1). Clause-level membership = the variant's DRS holding a should|must|may box anywhere: a consequent box emits its edge head, a condition box its operator goal, and no other source emits a should|must|may operator term (reserved names, `ace_to_pl.pl:2168–2192`) — P2 pins the equivalence per scene. In a scoped document every clause of a deontic group that is not an exclusion variant ends with `\+ guideline_exclusion(Key)`, after every other body goal (the sup1 D4 date goals included): edge clauses, payload clauses, wrappers, consequent entities + cardinalities, asserted consequents; a root deontic group's facts become rules whose body is the guard (the conditional root group). Exclusion variant (E1) = a variant the scope pass recognizes as an exclusion (cs1 D3): no clause of it takes the guard — record, `-` edge, box content — whatever its condition holds. Outside the guard: groups that are not deontic (byte-identical to today), exclusion variants, the document, schema + supersession records. Pass order unchanged: translation → `bf_order` (inside variant construction) → scope pass → supersession pass, so the guard stays last. The guard holds no variable: Deps, Skolems, ordinals, box numbers + variable numbering unchanged.
Semantics (REFERENCE): an exclusion whose condition holds blocks every statement of guideline `Key` that holds a should|must|may box — its edges and everything it derives (payload, wrappers, entities, asserted consequents); a statement without a box stays unblocked; an exclusion statement is never blocked by its own guideline's exclusion (it states it); an exclusion whose condition reads its own guideline's guarded box forms a negative cycle through that box's guard (TA refuses it where the cycle is live in TA's envelope); the absence of an exclusion is not proof of inclusion; a composition = one patient (cs1 D5); a cut negation → `indeterminate(limit)` (CS0).

D3 Superseded statements (amends sup1 D4 + D5). Selection (R1) = D2's deontic groups: a group holding a should|must|may edge head (sup1 D4), a rule whose box sits in its condition alone (newly dated) and a root deontic group (newly admitted); groups without a box (asserted rules, `can`-only claims, facts) stay undated (sup1 Out of scope). Each selected group gains on every clause the date goals `guideline_evaluation_date(V), guideline_satisfies(exactly, V, less, Date)` (before the D2 guard in a scoped document) and the witness pair V ↦ Date − 1; a root group's obligation = its first clause's positive goals under the witness = `guideline_evaluation_date(Date − 1)`, prove = its heads. Fork: `v1_group_obligation` reads the first clause's positive goals for every group kind (today `Kind == fact → Facts = []` drops the date witness: map R7; a plain fact group still yields `[]`), so the fork's proof payload = the kernel's (`ckc certify` refuses a mismatch: `fork_payload_mismatch.`). An exclusion variant whose condition holds a box is selected like any deontic group: every clause, its exclusion record included, takes the date goals (the record is a semantic predicate; the provenance records stay outside: consultant-20 C4); the scope guard stays off it (E1).

D4 Obligations of guarded groups. The scope guard is a NAF: it adds no witness fact, so obligation extraction at fixed witness pairs is unchanged. Witness selection (contract ws: the first candidate tuple whose obligation replays over the post-pass program) reads the guarded clauses ⇒ a formula rule whose replay reaches a newly guarded clause can select other pairs (consultant-20 C5's D/E/F lead, P3) — payload identity is graded per corpus (D8), never assumed; the fork payload = the kernel's always. A guard-only root group (scoped, unsuperseded) = witness([]) + prove(Heads), as a fact group today: its replay derives the heads unless the document's own clauses derive an exclusion of its key (an exclusion whose condition the document's own facts meet → `underivable_obligation`, an authoring error caught at compile; cs1 P4 precedent).

D5 Certification (spec `emit.rs` `project`, kernel `m6_*`; exact-equality postconditions as today). Mutant battery, each canonical (it reaches the projection or obligation decision, never a reader or launch error) + pinned reason (`clauses(S)` | `projection` | the obligation comparison | `fork_payload_mismatch.`): a guard dropped from any clause of a deontic group (edge, payload, wrapper, entity, root, condition-only); a guard added to a group without a box or to any clause of an exclusion variant; a guard on a record; a guard not last; guard polarity (`\+ guideline_exclusion(K)` → `guideline_exclusion(K)`); guard key changed; a duplicated guard; a date goal after the guard; date comparator `less` → `leq`; the date variable aliased with a body variable (canonical numbering kept); a superseded root group without date goals or with an altered witness date; a fork proof payload ≠ the kernel's. ## Rulings

- E1 Exclusion statements take no scope guard (MAIN; consultant-20 C5 + C12; the advisor; derived from the obligation code, not run). A recognized exclusion variant's obligation proves every head of the variant, its record included (`v1_group_obligation`, `ace_to_pl.pl:4858–4872`; `emit::obligation`, `emit.rs:4644–4663`); its witness asserts its condition, so its record derives and any guarded clause beside it fails ⇒ under any guard on the variant (record outside or inside) an exclusion whose condition holds a box fails its own replay, while Decisions admit a box in a rule condition. Exempting the whole variant is the only reading that admits it; it continues cs1 D3 ("Exclusion clauses carry no guard"), and a same-key loop through a guarded producer stays TA's refusal. Decisions amendment (Deontic conditions, Guard): "… document, schema + supersession records outside; an exclusion statement (Condition scope) takes no guard on any clause (its own obligation replays every head; a same-key loop through a guarded producer stays the typed analysis's refusal) …".
- E2 A box inside a `does not provably` condition stays `deferred_operator` (MAIN; consultant-20 C1): a translation boundary of every document, reached before the scope pass, which the Decisions' retired scope rejects never covered; admitting it = a separate translation surface (NAF-local context, witness + safety), not in DC. Decisions amendment (Deontic conditions, admission): "… (any operator inside a `does not provably` condition stays `deferred_operator`, in every document)".
- R1 Supersession selection (user ruling, AskUserQuestion at this contract: "Block it by default (Recommended)", picked over "Keep it deriving"): the date guard selects D2's deontic groups — a superseded statement holding a should|must|may box anywhere, a condition included, derives only under an earlier evaluation date; an exclusion variant holding a box is dated whole (C4); groups without a box keep deriving (sup1 Out of scope).
