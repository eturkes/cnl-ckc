## Definition (spec, `emit.rs`)

- `waited(t)`: the arguments of a positive goal whose variables must be bound before it runs — a comparison or arithmetic built-in (`compare::is_builtin`): its inputs (the variable inputs of emitted built-ins; a comparison's target half is ground in every emission) (`guideline_satisfies/4` args 1–2, `/6` args 1–3, `guideline_product/6` + `guideline_quotient/6` args 1–4); a semantic predicate of arity ≥ 2 other than `guideline_entity/4` + `guideline_event/3` (the noun/verb generators): its 2nd argument (the referent: entity, event or box context); anything else: none.
- `ready(item, bound, pv)`: `Pos(t)` ⇔ every variable of `waited(t)` ∈ `bound`; `Naf(gs)` ⇔ every variable of `gs` that occurs in a positive item of the body (`pv`) ∈ `bound`.
- `bind_first(body)`: repeatedly take the leftmost ready item of the remaining items (none ready ⇒ the leftmost item), append it, add the variables of a taken `Pos` item to `bound` (a `Naf` binds nothing); `bound` starts empty (an open call binds no head variable).
- Built-in invariant (projection property, stated separately: the safety checks alone do not establish it): in every emitted DRS-order body each built-in's waited variables occur in an earlier positive goal (comparison conditions + formula chains follow their quantities). Under it, and under NAF safety, the leftmost fallback never takes a built-in or a NAF item before its binders (its binders precede it in DRS order, so a leftmost unready built-in/NAF contradicts the premise).
- Emission: a rule group's clauses = `rule_clauses(heads, bind_first(goals + chain))`; the existing checks (NAF safety with body domains, head safety, antecedent/consequent presence) run unchanged on the DRS-order body `goals + chain`, so every reject + its order stays identical; obligation witness facts keep their definition (`positive_goals(clauses[0].body)`), i.e. follow the emitted order. Fact groups, the scope guard `\+ guideline_exclusion(Key)` (appended after the group, stays last) and question projections keep their order (questions out of scope).
