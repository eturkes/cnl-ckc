## Design

D1 Table. A v3 table accepts `approximation<TAB>A<TAB>about` (A = an adjective lemma that marks a time quantity approximate) and `range<TAB>P<TAB>minimum` (P = the preposition that joins the upper end of a ranged minimum). Row law = q12's + an approximation lemma occurs in at most one approximation row; a preposition occurs in at most one of the relation, frequency and range rows. Violations reject in `ckc check` + `ckc compile` with pinned messages `approximation value`, `range value`, `duplicate adjective`, `duplicate preposition` (+ ` at row N`). A v2 table keeps rejecting both kinds as `kind`.
D2 Claimed quantities + frames. Interval quantity (no frame); recurrence gap Q (frame = its spacing frame); scoped recurrence gap (frame = its spacing frame); window length L (frame = its window frame); frequency period (no frame); range upper end H (no frame; D4).
D3 About. A claimed quantity Q is approximate when a same-context `property(R, A, pos)` with A an approximation lemma has R = Q or R = Q's frame. An approximate Q compiles its `guideline_cardinality` clause with comparison `about` in place of `eq`; every other primitive — the property clause included — stays as v1 emits it. Rejects `temporal_shape(approximate_bound, S)` at the pattern's pp: an approximate interval quantity, recurrence gap or window length whose comparison ≠ `eq` (`for at least 2 approximate hours`). A frequency period keeps q12's rule (comparison ≠ `eq` → `frequency_shape`); an approximate exact period compiles `about`. An approximation property on any other referent stays a plain property. Denotation: about N units with no tolerance; matching stays structural (`about 2` matches only `about 2`, never `eq 2` or an inequality).
D4 Range. A `modifier_pp(E, P, H)` with P a range lemma and H a same-context time quantity is a range pp (H claimed); P with any other object stays a plain pp. Its partner = the one interval annotation on E in the same context whose quantity L has comparison `geq`, collected over the whole scope (the range pp may precede the interval pp). A paired range pp emits `guideline_range(Ctx, L, H)` right after it; the interval owns L, the range borrows L + owns H. Rejects `temporal_shape(range_shape, S)`: at the range pp — H comparison ≠ `eq`, H approximate, H with an `of` link (q12 bound rules first: `no_bound`, `zero_bound`); after the pass — no partner, ≥ 2 partners, partner unit ≠ H unit, L count ≥ H count, two range pps on one L. Denotation: ranged lower threshold — the stated minimum θ lies from L's count to H's count of the unit, the event's own bound stays `≥ θ`, and H bounds nothing about the event.
D5 Reject order. Pattern rejects at their own pp in q12's left-to-right pass; after the pass: range pairing (range pps in item order), then one range per L, then q12's shared claims, then q12's window requirement (first violation in item order).
D6 Questions. v3 question law = D3–D5 over the flattened goal items (same recognizers + rejects); the q12 version matrix stands.
D7 Certification. emit.rs v3 projection derives `about` + `guideline_range` from the DRS + the raw table; a document whose `about` or range the DRS does not justify fails certification (q12's `doc-v3-about` + `doc-v3-range` keep their pins).
D8 UI (K5). The comparison `about` renders `about ` in every timing phrase (`lasts about 2 hours`, `repeats about 2 hours apart`, `about 4 weeks after`, `per about 1 day`, windows). The interval row of a range's low end L renders its bound as `a minimum of N to M <unit>` (N = L's count, M = H's count; unit word singular iff M = 1) in place of `at least N <unit>`; a join that fails renders `not stated`; the `guideline_range` literal renders no row of its own (one enriched row, consultant C9). Time words: an approximation row reads `marks a time limit as approximate`; a range row reads `joins the upper end of a minimum that is stated as a range`. Fixed strings append to the copy table. Pages of documents that `ckc compile` produced under v1/v2 stay byte-identical (v1/v2 compiles never emit `about`); the reader stays permissive (consultant C8), so a hand-edited v1/v2 pl with an `about` comparison renders `about ` like a v3 page (MAIN ruling on reviewer-3 R4-F1).
D9 Corpus. `temporal.tsv` + `approximation approximate about` + `range to minimum`. Re-author s59-06 (`… at an approximate interval of 2 hours`), rec07-imp03 (a follow-up `after 4 approximate weeks of a therapy-start`), s40-01 (`wait for at least 8 hours to 12 hours after the last-dose before the first-buprenorphine-dose`; ER/LA `… 12 hours to 24 hours …`), s59-12 (`should last for at least 7 days to 10 days`), s47-16 (`for at least 4 days to 7 days`, `… 5 days to 7 days`); align + projection-notes rows updated; `ckc certify cdc-2022-opioid` green; queries re-derived; review-manifest + release-manifest regenerated.
