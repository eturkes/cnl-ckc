## Design

D1 Evidence (ruling 1). In `source/rec01-05-body-extraction.txt`, region S33-01 becomes the whole PDF p33 table with its notes, one payload in source order: the title; the header + 10 rows (`<cell>: <cell>`, rows joined by `; `, a header convention line); the `*` footnote (the multiply instruction, the two worked examples, the caution lead-in, cautions 1–7 with their printed `1)`–`7)` labels); the `†` tapentadol + `§` tramadol notes. Sources + abbreviations under the table stay scaffolding (citations, unit glosses). Locator section = `Rec 4 > Supporting rationale > Table`. Regions S33-02..S33-06 merge into it (locators + payloads removed); S33-07 stays. Census line 309 → 304.

D2 The document `cdc2022-opioid-s33-01` projects every statement of the region:
- 10 conversion rules, one per row, factor = the row's printed spelling (`0.15`, `2.4`, `1.0`, `5.0`, `4.7`, `1.0`, `1.5`, `3.0`, `0.4`, `0.2`): `If some <opioid>-milligrams-per-day X are contained by an opioid-dosage and the opioid-dosage does not provably serve an opioid-use-disorder-management then the opioid-dosage includes some MME-units-per-day Y and Y = X * <factor>.` (the amount leads: ruling 3); fentanyl reads `transdermal-fentanyl-micrograms-per-hour` (caution 1); the absence condition = caution 7 (an absence condition: no record of OUD management; Decisions `Condition scope` authoring) — no MME derives for a dosage recorded as serving that management. The multiply instruction = these rules (the old duty sentence retires).
- Worked examples (ruling 2): per example, an example-only regimen noun (never `opioid-dosage`: a root example fact would match every rule over that noun), root example facts with the stated amounts, a formula rule deriving the daily total (amount per dose × doses per day) and one deriving its MME (× the row's factor); results = the stated totals (20 mg + 20 MME; 20 mg + 30 MME); acetaminophen 325 mg = a stated amount outside MME.
- Cautions 3, 4, 5, 7 = the sentences of the retired documents `cdc2022-opioid-s33-03`..`-s33-06`, kept (caution 7 = its prohibition sentence beside the absence condition: no loss of either reading); cautions 2 + 6 + the `†`/`§` classifications = plain claims where APE parses them; each omitted part (rationales: relation-structure class; "unknown whether …": probability class) → the projection-notes row.
- Verb convention (contract da2 D9): an opioid dosage `contains` its drug amount and `includes` its MME amount; every formula rule's result verb is absent from its own condition, so no rule derives its own condition's event.
