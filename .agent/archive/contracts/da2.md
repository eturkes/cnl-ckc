## Design

D1 Surface. A formula = a `formula(Y, =, E)` condition at the top level of a rule's final consequent (after `curry`; every Horn variant); nowhere else (root sentence, antecedent, operator box, NAF payload, question). Its parts:
- Target Y = a referent of a consequent top-level `object(Y, Noun, countable, na, geq, 2)` whose noun token (the object's anchor index T) is preceded by the token `some` (index T − 1; no adjective between). One formula per target; a target is never an operand.
- E = a tree of `expr(*, L, R)` and `expr(/, L, R)` (APE's left-associative parse, parentheses included); leaves = operand referents or literals `int(N)` | `real(F)`. APE parses `(X * 3) / 2` as `expr(/,expr(*,B,int(3)),int(2))` and both `-1` and `- 1` as `int(-1)` (tokens `-1` | `-`, `1`); a signed numeral rejects `literal` (D2).
- Operand = a referent of an antecedent `object(R, Noun, countable, na, C, N)` at the antecedent's top level in every variant (a shared condition: not inside an operator box, a NAF payload or a disjunction arm), not a time quantity (no `unit` row for its noun), whose determiner is `some` (`C = geq, N = 2` + the token `some` before the noun: no bound, extension 2) or a comparison (`C ∈ {exactly, geq, greater, leq, less}` read as nt1 reads it: `at least 2` is `geq 2`, filtered).
Extension 1 (a unit-noun amount stands for its count) is the reading, not a check: units stay nouns; no unit algebra.

D2 Literal values (consultant-1 F4). A literal's value comes from its source lexeme, never from APE's float:
- The driver prints every float token as `'$decimal'(Text)`, Text = an atom holding the ASCII bytes `write_term/2` prints for the float under the envelope's own options (SWI 9.2.9: the shortest round-trip spelling), e.g. `'$decimal'('1.5')`; the term grammar is unchanged; every other byte of the dump stays as today (D8). `'$decimal'` passes the reserved-name scan (`reserved_atom` = the `guideline_` | `$guideline_` prefixes) and occurs nowhere else: no ACE fixture holds a decimal numeral (`rg '[0-9]\.[0-9]'` over every `*.ace` | `*.ace.in` under `guidelines/` + `tests/`: 0 hits; the 3 hits in `tests/v1/*/input.txt.in` are codec inputs that never reach the driver).
- Numeric lexemes of a raw ACE line = the maximal runs `[0-9]+(\.[0-9]+)?` whose preceding byte is not an ASCII letter, digit, `-`, `_` or `.`, and whose following byte is not an ASCII letter, digit, `-` or `_` (so `X1`, `covid-19` and `2-week` hold none). The numeric tokens of the sentence's token list (integers and `'$decimal'(Text)`) correspond to the line's numeric lexemes one-to-one, in order; each pair must spell alike: an integer token = its decimal digits; a decimal token = Text. A sentence holding a formula with a different count or any differing pair rejects (`literal`). Consequences: `1.50`, `02`, `0.10000000000000000000000001`, `0.00001` (printed `1.0e-5`) reject; `1.5`, `0.15`, `2.4`, `4.7`, `5`, a 30-digit integer pass.
- In a sentence holding a formula, a `-` or `+` token directly before a numeric token rejects (`literal`): `- 1`, `(- 1.5)`, `- 0`, `-1`.
- The literal leaves of E, in in-order (token) order, pair with the first numeric tokens after the formula's anchor token; each leaf's dump spelling (its integer as printed, sign included; the Text of `'$decimal'(Text)`) equals its token's (else `literal`: `int(-1)` against token `1`), and its lexeme is followed by the line's end, a space, `)`, `*`, `/` or the final `.` (else `literal`: APE reads `50%` as `int(50)` and drops the `%`). Each leaf takes its lexeme's exact value: `I` → I; `I.F` → (I·10^|F| + F) / 10^|F|, canonical (DA1 D1: `canon`). Value 0 is legal data (a 0 divisor rejects at D4).

D3 Compiled form (per variant clause; the variant's other law unchanged):
- Pass order (spec + fork): nt1's conversion (`compared` | `nt1_compare`) runs first and converts every convertible antecedent object, a `some` operand's `(geq, 2)` included; the formula pass runs after it on the converted items. A `some` operand = nt1's item for that referent minus its `guideline_satisfies` goal (`guideline_entity(Ctx, R, Noun, countable)`, `guideline_cardinality(Ctx, R, Unit, S, N)`; extension 2); a comparison operand keeps nt1's item whole. Either way the operand pair = nt1's cvar (S, N) of that referent (its V slot unused).
- Chain: E in postorder; each node `expr(Op, L, R)` → `guideline_product(SL, NL, SR, NR, S', N')` (`*`) | `guideline_quotient(…)` (`/`) with fresh S', N'; an operand leaf = its pair; a literal leaf = `(exactly, value)`. The chain stands after every antecedent goal of the clause body (after NAF goals; before the cs1 guard, which stays last on edge clauses), formulas in DRS order.
- Head: the target's cardinality head = `guideline_cardinality(Ctx', Sk(Y), Unit, S_root, N_root)` (the chain root's outputs) in place of the verbatim `geq, 2`; every other head unchanged. Every clause of the rule carries the same body (one body per variant, as today).
- Fresh variables: chain outputs follow nt1's block — node i (formulas in DRS order, nodes in postorder) owns cb + 3·|nt1 referents| + 2i and + 2i + 1 — never a Deps, Skolem or witness identity slot (printed names come from per-line canonical renumbering, so the indices fix identity, not bytes); `head_safe` holds (S_root, N_root are chain outputs); `naf_safe` unchanged (chain variables never enter a payload).
Worked example: the measured sentence ⇒ body `…, guideline_entity(actual,B,'oxycodone-milligram-per-day',countable), guideline_cardinality(actual,B,na,C,D), guideline_event(actual,E,contain), …, guideline_product(C,D,exactly,rational(3,2),F,G)`, head `guideline_cardinality(actual,'$guideline_id'(product,…,ref(4),…),na,F,G)`.

D4 Witness + obligation. Operand witness = an exact nonzero value its condition admits where one exists: `some` → (exactly, 1); `exactly N` → (exactly, N); `geq N` → N, or 1 when N = 0; `leq N` → N, or −1 when N = 0; `greater N` → N + 1, or N + 2 when N + 1 = 0; `less N` → N − 1, or N − 2 when N − 1 = 0 (formula operands only; other converted conditions keep nt1 D4). Chain outputs ↦ DA1 `arith_result` over the witness-bound inputs, node by node; a node that derives nothing (an `exactly 0` operand or a literal 0 as divisor) rejects (`witness`). Facts = positive body goals minus every built-in, under the witness; heads = bound heads (the target's cardinality ground at the computed pair) ⇒ the obligation stays modus ponens over the document's own clauses and replays in K2 (DA1 engine) unchanged.

D5 Rejects: `formula_shape(Why, S)`, class `unsupported`, first failing check per formula in DRS order, checks in this order: `position` (a formula outside D1's place) → `relation` (an operator other than `=`) → `operator` (a node other than `*`, `/`, or a bare leaf `Y = X` | `Y = 1.5`: a formula holds at least one node) → `target` (D1 target law, incl. a second formula on it, a target used as an operand, a non-referent left side) → `operand` (over every leaf of the formula before any leaf's `operand_bound`: a leaf referent that is not a top-level antecedent countable count of every variant: a consequent, box, NAF or arm referent, a time quantity (a plain-number one too), a measurement (Unit ≠ `na`), a mass or `dom` object) → `operand_bound` (a top-level antecedent countable object whose determiner is neither `some` + plural nor a comparison: a plain number, `a`/`an` (`eq, 1`), `about`, `na`) → `literal` (D2) → `witness` (D4). Question mode keeps `query_unsupported(leaf(formula/3), S)`.

D6 Certification (`emit.rs` `project` + kernel `m6_*`): the projection derives D1–D5 from the DRS, the dump's token lists and the raw ACE lines (`certify_doc` passes them); a changed, dropped, added or misplaced chain goal, a wrong literal value, operand link, operator or head pair, a kept `geq, 2` head or a satisfies filter on a `some` operand fails as `clauses(S)`; a formula the projection rejects certifies nothing (`unsupported(sentence(S, formula_shape(Why)))`, the certifier's spelling of the fork's `formula_shape(Why, S)`, as for `temporal_shape`). Certification takes no payload input: it derives the D4 payload (`certify-one` prints it; `certify` feeds it to the aggregate replay), so the D4 witness choice is pinned by byte equality in the tests (P4), and an underivable supplied head fails `obligation_failed` in the replay.

D7 Fork (`ace_to_pl.pl`): mirrors D1–D5 (formula pass after `nt1_compare`, `v1_witness_pairs` extended); the per-document replay module asserts the full DA1 schema clauses (`nt1_schema_clauses` → the `guidelines/schema.pl` clause list: rational comparisons, `guideline_product/6`, `guideline_quotient/6`, helpers); a test pins the list to the clauses read from `schema_bytes()`, element by element in order, each pair variant-equal (`=@=`; the list shares variables across elements, so a whole-list comparison is false), with dropped, reordered and altered-clause plants red. Header names contract da2.

D8 Driver (`rust/ckc/prolog/drs_dump.pl`, trusted): before printing, every float in the sentences + DRS terms becomes `'$decimal'(Text)` (D2); integers, atoms, structure, variable numbering and the three-line envelope unchanged ⇒ every existing dump byte-identical (no float occurs in the corpus); `ckc trust-audit --write` re-pins it.

D9 Termination: no new check. A formula rule whose condition and result share a verb can feed itself operationally (REFERENCE § Arithmetic built-ins, DA1); the authoring convention (conversion rules read `contains`, derive `includes`) lives in DA3's corpus rules; a cycle scan over formula nouns = a deferred row with its acceptance check (`.agent/deferred.md`).

D10 Docs: REFERENCE § Arithmetic built-ins gains the ACE surface (position, target, operands, both extensions, literal spelling law, witness, rejects); `compiler.md` (formula pass, replay clauses, driver decimals); compiler header.

## Predicates (acceptance; seed-red unless marked `already-passing`, those credited by mutation or proof)

- P1 surface: the D1 shapes compile (rc 0) with D3 bytes against an independent model: one product with a decimal literal; a quotient; a chain of 2–3 nodes with parentheses; two operands (`X * Z`); a literal-only formula; a comparison-determiner operand (filter kept + operand pair), incl. `exactly` | `less` (APE's condition-list wrapper); a curried rule; a Horn-split rule with the operand in the shared conditions; two formulas with distinct targets; a target also named by another consequent literal; an antecedent NAF goal (the chain after it); a scoped document (scope + exclusion table) whose formula rule also has a modal consequent sibling (the chain before the cs1 guard); an unrelated `some` quantity beside an operand (its `geq 2` filter + nt1 witness kept).
- P2 literal law: the D2 correspondence over lines with integers elsewhere in the sentence (`at least 50 …` + a formula), variables `X1`, hyphenated words with digits, a 30-digit integer, `0.15`; the rejecting spellings `1.50`, `02`, `0.10000000000000000000000001`, `0.00001`, `-1`, `- 1`, `(- 1.5)`, `- 0`, `50%` → `formula_shape(literal, S)`; mutants that drop the sign or the suffix check each accept one of them; a synthetic dump (a `certify-one` input) whose formula leaf differs from its unchanged token + lexeme (`real('$decimal'('2.5'))` against `1.5`) kills a mutant without the leaf ↔ token check.
- P3 rejects: each D5 `Why` fires on its minimal sentence, and the first-error order holds on sentences failing two checks.
- P4 witness + payload: the obligation bytes (operand witness values, computed head pair, facts without built-ins) for each P1 shape: the test model's payload = the fork's `proof` output = `certify-one`'s stdout byte for byte, and a coherent witness + head mutation (witness 1 → 2, head 3/2 → 3) in any one of them breaks the equality while the replay still proves it; nonzero witnesses for `less than 1` and `at least 0` divisors (accepted ACE); `greater -1` + `leq 0` = kernel-level witness-function cases (APE admits no negative cardinality); an `exactly 0` divisor operand and a literal 0 divisor reject `witness`; `ckc v1 aggregate-check` over the compiled documents → ok; a payload head with another value → `obligation_failed`.
- P5 certification: `ckc certify-one` accepts every P1 document; PL mutants (literal value, operand swap, `*`↔`/`, dropped / extra / moved chain goal, head pair constant or `geq, 2`, a satisfies filter added to a `some` operand, operand variable relinked) each reject `clauses(S)`.
- P6 end to end (native engine, hand-sized compositions of compiled formula documents + patient facts): `exactly 40` oxycodone mg/day → `exactly 60` MME → a `geq 50` threshold rule fires; `geq 40` → `geq 60`; codeine `exactly 333.3` × 0.15 → `exactly rational(9999,200)` (49.995) → the threshold does not fire; transdermal fentanyl per mcg/hr × 2.4; traces carry `builtin(guideline_product(…))` leaves.
- P7 fork replay clauses = `schema_bytes()` clause list (test + planted red); per-document replay of every P1 document passes.
- P8 driver: a float token prints `'$decimal'(Text)`; every corpus + fixture dump byte-identical to the pre-DA2 driver (already-passing on float-free inputs, credited by the byte comparison + mutation of the new branch).
- P9 no regression (already-passing at the seed, credited by byte comparison + the mutation run): the corpus + 6 queries byte-identical; every red/green probe + certify case unchanged.
- P10 gates: `just gate` green; `just verify` 0/0; trust re-pinned; each test labelled seed-red or already-passing.

## Rulings (MAIN, during the unit)

- Consequent NAF: a NAF box in a rule consequent rejects while the rule flattens (fork `forbidden_operator(consequent, naf, S)`, certifier `naf_placement`), formula or not, before the formula pass (D3 pass order); D5 ranks the formula checks among themselves. tester-2's p3 synthetic `~` case expects `naf_placement` (reviewer-2 R6/R12: accepted).
- reviewer-2 findings (target d58035ad; `.agent/review.md` DA2 rows) bind the fix: F1 = D5 checks every leaf's `operand` law (top-level antecedent, countable, Unit `na`, no time quantity: an unconverted plain-number unit-row noun carries `'$guideline_unit'`) before any leaf's `operand_bound`; F2 = an operand's Unit slot must be `na` (an APE measurement such as `at least 2 kg of apples X` rejects `operand`; widening D1 to measurements needs a user ruling); F3 = REFERENCE states the `some` immediately-before-noun limit + the countable / no-time operand limits, and its ACE example is one physical line.
- G1 (register): D4's fixed witness can violate an antecedent NAF goal (`… does not provably contain at least 1 cat …` ⇒ fork `underivable_obligation`, native aggregate `obligation_failed`) — a completeness limit outside D1–D5, queued in `.agent/deferred.md` (fixed-witness NAF completeness).
