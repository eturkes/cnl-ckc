# SUP2 supersession census — CDC 2022 opioid guideline (927 regions)

Criterion (research briefs = `.scratch/sup/research-brief.md` shape, frozen here): `superseded` = after 2022-11-04 (publication) a law, regulation or guidance from an authority with standing (Congress, DEA, FDA, HHS, SAMHSA, CMS, the CDC itself, or the publisher of a document the region reports) took effect and replaces, eliminates or contradicts the specific fact, requirement or recommendation the region states, so the statement no longer holds as stated from that date; `not-superseded` = no such change, or a change that adds options without contradiction, or a historical report still true; `pre-publication` = a change before publication the region misses (a guideline error, never a supersession); `unclear` = evidence does not decide. Effective date = legal or operative effect, never publication/proposal/compliance dates.

Method: two blind triage passes over every region (`triage-1.tsv` 243 candidates, `triage-2.tsv` 308; validator `check-triage.py`) → union `candidates.tsv` 314 (237 both + 6 pass-1 only + 71 pass-2 only) → four topic clusters (`cluster-*.tsv`: law 77, fda 84, federal 56, society 97) researched against fetched primary text (`research-*.tsv`; validator `check-research.py`; verbatim quotes + URLs per row) → MAIN rulings below. Regions flagged `clear` by both passes (613) = not superseded by the triage rule (no dependency a later change could supersede).

Results: superseded 5 (`rows.tsv`, the SUP3 table rows); research verdicts 303 not-superseded, 1 pre-publication, 5 unclear (law 2, fda 2, society 1).
