# Deferral queue

Off-path improvements + ruled deferrals, one row each: `- <what> (<source>). Accept: <check>`. A row enters while its evidence is fresh and leaves in the commit that meets its check; a skipped, xfailed, deleted or tier-demoted case enters here first, with its reason + the check that restores it (`CLAUDE.md` `Engineering` Verification integrity); a gate bug (`CLAUDE.md` `Execution` Gate usefulness: a gate defect or flake) enters with its ledger class, its fix the next unit. Unfinished units stay in `.agent/spec.md` `Tasks`.
